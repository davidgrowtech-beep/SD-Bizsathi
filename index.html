Kya aap ek hi Facebook account se multiple clients ke Meta Ads chala sakte hain?

Haan, kar sakte hain. Do tareeke hain. (1) Recommended: client apna Facebook Page aur Ad Account hamari Business Manager me partner access se jodta hai. Ad spend client ke payment method se seedha Meta ko jata hai, Pixel aur data client ke naam rehta hai, aur ek Business Manager se kai clients sambhal sakte hain. (2) Hamare apne ad account/page se ads chalana bhi possible hai, par risk zyada hai: ek client ki policy violation se sabke ads ruk sakte hain, trading/finance aur health jaise restricted categories me jaldi disable hone ka khatra rehta hai, aur ad spend alag se collect karke handle karna padta hai. Isliye hum option 1 prefer karte hain.

Ad spend kaun pay karta hai?

Daily ad budget Meta ko client direct pay karta hai. Hamare charges (GST ke saath) uske alawa hain.

Kam se kam daily budget kitna rakhein?

Suggestion: shuruaat me ₹300–500 per day, taaki Meta ko data mile. Final budget aapke area, offer aur competition par depend karta hai.

Kya leads ki guarantee milti hai?

Nahi. Leads ki sankhya aur cost offer, creative, budget aur market par depend karti hai. Hum campaign ko behtar banane ka kaam karte hain.

Kya har business ke ads chal sakte hain?

Trading/finance, clinic/health aur kuch dusri categories par Meta ki sakht policies hain (claims, disclaimers, targeting restrictions). Meta ki policies badalti rehti hain, isliye latest rules Meta Business Help Center par verify karein.

Telegram campaign me landing page milta hai?

Haan, Telegram leads packages me landing page shamil hai. Pro package me custom branded page aur Pixel setup hota hai.
